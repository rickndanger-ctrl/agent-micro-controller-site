Where this stands

Glasses support is an experimental, do-it-yourself add-on. It works, but it isn't a one-tap setup yet: the glasses app is not published in Even Hub, and each Mac needs a small bridge service that you install yourself. Everything else in Agent Micro Controller works without it.

The glasses only display. Dictation, review and Send always happen in Agent Micro Controller on your iPhone or iPad; nothing is sent from the glasses.

What you need

  • Even Realities G2 glasses, connected to the Even app on an iPhone.
  • Agent Micro Controller on your iPhone or iPad, paired with your Mac.
  • Tailscale on the iPhone running the Even app, and on each Mac. The glasses reach your Macs only through Tailscale, even when the controller itself pairs over Wi-Fi.
  • On each Mac: Node 22.13 or newer and the Genesis G2 bridge from the source code below.
  • Comfort with the Terminal. Building the glasses app needs Even's developer tools.

Set it up

  1. In Agent Micro Controller, open Settings and turn on Even G2 glasses (experimental). The Follow controls appear below the controller.
  2. On each Mac, follow the Genesis G2 README to build the glasses app, install its bridge and give the bridge a Tailscale HTTPS address.
  3. Show that Mac's G2 pairing code, then in the Even app open Even Hub, use the developer control at the top right, and scan it. The glasses app opens and pairs with the Mac. Treat the code like a password.

Use it

  1. Open the glasses app inside Even on the iPhone, with the glasses connected.
  2. In Agent Micro Controller, choose the same Mac and agent, then turn on Follow this controller.
  3. Tap a chat key. The glasses follow it, show your draft as you dictate, and show the reply. Send from the controller as usual.

Only one iPhone or iPad controls the glasses at a time; taking over from another device is an explicit choice.

If the glasses stop updating

  • Check Tailscale on the iPhone first. If the glasses app says it can't reach the computer, open Tailscale on the phone. If it's off, turn it on. If it says Connected but shows a warning, switch it off and back on: Tailscale can look connected while passing no traffic.
  • Make sure the Mac you picked in the glasses app is awake and online. It does not switch to another Mac by itself.
  • Make sure the glasses are connected in the Even app. If they aren't, that's between the glasses and Even, not this app.
  • If another iPhone or iPad holds Follow, turn Follow off there or take over.

Source code

The glasses app and bridge are open source under the MIT licence: github.com/rickndanger-ctrl/genesis-g2. Questions or problems: open an issue.

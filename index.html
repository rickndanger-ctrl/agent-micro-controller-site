A little help.

Agent Micro Controller brings your Mac’s supported AI apps to a control deck on your iPhone or iPad. Here’s how to keep everything connected.

Start here

Back in control.

Choose the Mac and app you mean to use first. Then try the check that matches what you’re seeing.

Pair a Mac

Open the Mac companion and show its pairing code. In the controller’s Settings, choose Scan pairing code. Keep Tailscale connected on both devices. Treat pairing codes like passwords.

The Mac says disconnected

Make sure the companion is running and the Mac is awake. Check Tailscale on both devices, select the intended Mac, and refresh. Open Settings → Check this Mac for more detail.

A chat or action is missing

Open the selected AI app on your Mac, then refresh the controller. Check that the Mac companion has Accessibility permission in macOS Settings. Check this Mac can help identify unavailable controls.

Hold to talk needs attention

Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in your device’s Settings. Hold to dictate, release to review your draft, and press Send when ready. Dictation requires on-device speech recognition support.

A Send result is uncertain

Look in the selected chat on your Mac before trying again. A message may have arrived even when its confirmation could not be read. Checking first helps avoid sending the same prompt twice.

Remove a paired device

In the Mac companion’s paired-device list, choose Revoke. Removing the iPhone or iPad app alone does not revoke its access. Older shared credentials have a separate legacy-access control.

Something else?

Tell us what happened.

Report a problem or ask a question through the public support repository. Privacy questions and removal requests can go there too—without the sensitive details.

Open support on GitHub ↗

A useful report includes

  • Your app and operating system versions.
  • The control or feature you tried.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.

GitHub issues are public. Don’t post pairing codes, credentials, private chats, or unredacted screenshots. Remove sensitive information before sharing.
